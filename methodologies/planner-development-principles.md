# Planner Development Principles

For software-project creation or substantial modification, choose the smallest
maintainable structure that follows the selected ecosystem's established
conventions.

When responsibilities can evolve independently, name their coherent modules or
artifacts and their integration relationships explicitly in the Worker
objective. Keep the connected set in one Worker outcome, but do not leave the
topology implicit or collapse materially distinct responsibilities into one
monolithic artifact merely because the platform permits it. Keep a small
cohesive unit together when separation would add no maintainability value.
